Select a green build pad on your bottom lane to build a cannon. Click an existing cannon to upgrade it.

You defend the BLUE bottom lane; the AI defends the RED top lane. Each side receives identical automatic waves from the left. Surviving invaders damage the defending fort on the right. First fort destroyed loses. You earn 20 credits every 5 seconds plus kill rewards. Waves arrive every 40 seconds. Use Speed 3× for faster testing. This is a self-contained browser proof of concept; it does not yet use the Unity game's assets or combat engine.
